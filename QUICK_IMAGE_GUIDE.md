# 🖼️ Quick Image Upload Guide

## 📸 Add Images to Your Blog/News in 3 Clicks!

### Where is the Image Button?

When you're editing content, look for this toolbar:

```
[B] [I] [U] [Strike] | [📝 List] [• List] | [🔗 Link] [🚩 Anchor] | [🖼️ IMAGE] ← HERE!
```

---

## 🚀 Upload Image (Super Simple!)

### Step 1: Click in Content
Click where you want your image to appear

### Step 2: Click Image Button (🖼️)
Look for the picture icon in the toolbar

### Step 3: Upload Tab
A dialog box opens:
- Click **"Upload"** tab at the top
- Click **"Choose File"** button
- Select your image
- Click **"Send it to the Server"**

### Step 4: Done! ✅
Your image appears in the content!

---

## 📝 Don't Forget!

After uploading, add **Alt Text** for SEO:
1. Click the image
2. Right-click → **Image Properties**
3. Fill **"Alternative Text"** field
4. Example: "MEP Engineering Dubai Project"

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Good Image Names
- ✅ `mep-services-dubai.jpg`
- ✅ `project-completion-2024.jpg`
- ❌ `IMG_1234.jpg`

### Compress Before Upload
Visit: https://tinypng.com
- Reduces file size by 70%
- Faster loading
- Better SEO

### Recommended Sizes
- Blog images: **800-1200px wide**
- Diagrams: **600-800px wide**
- Keep under **1MB** file size

## 📂 Your Images Location

Uploaded images are saved in:
```
/media/uploads/
```

You can reuse them in other posts by clicking:
**"Browse Server"** in the Image dialog

---

## 🆘 Not Working?

### Can't see Image button?
- Refresh page: Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows)
- Make sure you're in the **Content** field

### Upload fails?
- Check image size (max 5MB)
- Use JPG or PNG format
- Try compressing at TinyPNG.com

### Image not showing?
- Check if Django server is running
- Clear browser cache
- Make sure URL starts with `/media/`
