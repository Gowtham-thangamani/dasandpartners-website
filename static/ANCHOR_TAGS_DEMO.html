🎯 What Are Anchor Tags?

Anchor tags are like bookmarks in your article. They let readers jump directly to a specific section without scrolling through the entire page.

Example: When you click a link in the table of contents, it instantly takes you to that section. That's the magic of anchor tags!

They're essential for:

  • Long articles and blog posts
  • Technical documentation
  • Project updates with multiple sections
  • Service descriptions with categories

🛠️ How to Create Anchor Tags

Creating anchor tags in CKEditor is super easy! You don't need to know HTML - the visual editor does it for you.

Two Simple Methods:

Method 1: Using CKEditor Buttons

  1. Select your heading text
  2. Click the "Anchor" button (🚩 flag icon)
  3. Give it a name (e.g., "introduction")
  4. Click OK

Method 2: Using HTML (Advanced)

<h2 id="my-section">My Section Title</h2> <a href="#my-section">Jump to My Section</a>

📝 Step-by-Step Tutorial

1Write Your Content

Start by writing your article with clear sections:

<h2>Introduction</h2> <p>Welcome to our guide...</p> <h2>Main Content</h2> <p>Here's the detailed information...</p> <h2>Conclusion</h2> <p>To summarize...</p>

2Add ID Attributes

In CKEditor, click "Source" button and add IDs to your headings:

<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2> <p>Welcome to our guide...</p> <h2 id="main-content">Main Content</h2> <p>Here's the detailed information...</p> <h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h2> <p>To summarize...</p>

3Create Your Table of Contents

At the top of your article, add links to these sections:

<h3>Table of Contents</h3> <ul> <li><a href="#introduction">Introduction</a></li> <li><a href="#main-content">Main Content</a></li> <li><a href="#conclusion">Conclusion</a></li> </ul>

4Test It!

Save your post and preview it. Click the links - they should jump to the sections!

🎓 Best Practices

✅ Do's

  • Use descriptive anchor names (e.g., "mep-services" not "section1")
  • Keep anchor names lowercase
  • Use hyphens instead of spaces (e.g., "our-team")
  • Add "Back to Top" links in long sections
  • Test all anchor links before publishing
  • Create table of contents for articles over 1000 words

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't use spaces in anchor names
  • Don't use special characters (except hyphens)
  • Don't forget the # symbol in links
  • Don't use the same ID twice on one page
  • Don't make anchor names too long
  • Don't skip sections in your table of contents
Pro Tip: Always include a "Back to Top" link at the end of each major section for the best user experience!
