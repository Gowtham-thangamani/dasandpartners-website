About Das And Partners

Multidisciplinary Engineering Excellence

Das And Partners Engineering Consultancy is multidisciplinary Engineering Consultant in UAE, with a team of 100+ Engineers across corporate offices in Dubai, Abu Dhabi & Sharjah having a client base of ADNOC, Borouge, Tabreed, MCC, ITC, ENOC, Emarat Petroleum, Petrofac, Veolia, Rotary, HQC, Aldar, Dubai Holding, Veolia, VTTI, Etihad Airways, Abu Dhabi Airport, Dubai International Airport, Shell, BP, Taqa, Lulu, Engie Cofely Energy, TotalEnergies, Omniyat, & several other prestigious Clients. With a strong footprint of working in Projects across Abu Dhabi, Ruwais, Sila, Habshan, Gayathi, CICPA Refinery Areas, Al Ain, Dubai, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Ajman, Fujairah & Umm Al Quwain, we deliver end-to-end engineering services and our specialization includes wide range of service from concept design & feasibility studies to FEED, pre-FEED, HAZID, HAZOP, authority approvals, site supervision, Project Management, EPCM, PM-CM, Tendering, Costing, Contract Administration, Structural Strengthening, Evaluation, Retrofitting, & advanced BIM solutions. We cater to both Greenfield & Brownfield projects across various sectors such as Oil & Gas, Power Generation, Petrochemical, Marine & Offshore, Warehouses, Infrastructure, District Cooling, Roads, Wet & Dry Utilities, Bridges, Interchanges, Viaducts, Tunnels, Underpasses, Underground, Transport Structures, Process & Non-Process Plant Buildings, Blast Resistant Structures, Hydrocarbon Tanks, Hydraulic Structures, Horizontal Vessel, Onshore Piperacks / sleepers, equipment foundations, shelters, substations, tank farms, packaged buildings (E-house, analyzer shelters), modular pipe racks, Offshore structures (topsides + substructure) Fixed 4-leg jackets, tripods, GBS (gravity-based), compliant towers, Floating FPSO/FSO, semi-submersible, spar, TLP Modules on topsides, process, utilities, power gen, LQ, flare/vent, E-house Subsea, manifolds, PLEM/PLET, risers, umbilicals, Skid Package, Industrial, Airport, Commercial Projects including Mixed Use, High Rise Buildings, Recreational Facilities & Luxury Residential Buildings / Villas / Mansions.
