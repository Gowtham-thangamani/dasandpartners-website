Architect of Record

As the Architect of Record, Das And Partners (Das and Partners) Engineering Consultancy in Abu Dhabi assumes a pivotal role in overseeing the architectural aspects of a project from conception to completion.

The Architect of Record (AOR) is the licensed architect or architectural firm officially designated as responsible for the architectural design and documentation of a building project. The AOR holds the primary responsibility for the design and oversees its execution during the construction phase.

Architect of Record in Abu Dhabi

As the Architect of Record (AOR) at Das And Partners (Das and Partners) Engineering Consultancy in Abu Dhabi, we take pride in our commitment to excellence and innovation in architectural design and engineering services. Our AOR services encompass a wide range of responsibilities aimed at ensuring the successful realization of architectural projects from conception to completion. Here's an overview of what our AOR services entail:

Project Management:

As the AOR, we serve as the primary point of contact for the client and oversee all aspects of the architectural project. We manage project timelines, budgets, and resources to ensure that the project stays on track and meets client expectations.

Design Coordination:

We collaborate closely with other consultants, including structural engineers, MEP engineers, and interior designers, to coordinate the various aspects of the design process. Our goal is to ensure that all design elements are integrated seamlessly and that the final design meets the client's objectives.

Regulatory Compliance:

We ensure that the architectural design complies with local building codes, regulations, and standards. We work closely with regulatory authorities to obtain necessary permits and approvals for the project and address any compliance issues that may arise.

Quality Assurance:

We implement rigorous quality assurance processes to ensure that the architectural design meets the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship. We conduct regular inspections and reviews throughout the design and construction phases to identify and address any quality issues promptly.

Construction Administration:

During the construction phase, we provide ongoing support and supervision to ensure that the project is built according to the approved design and specifications. We review contractor submittals, conduct site inspections, and address any construction-related issues that may arise.

Client Communication:

We maintain open and transparent communication with the client throughout the project lifecycle. We provide regular updates on project progress, address any concerns or questions that the client may have, and ensure that their feedback is incorporated into the design process.

Post-Construction Services:

After the completion of the project, we provide post-construction services such as occupancy evaluations and warranty inspections to ensure that the building performs as intended. We address any post-construction issues promptly and work with the client to resolve any warranty-related issues.
