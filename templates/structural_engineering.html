Structural Engineering involves the analysis, design, and construction of structures to ensure they can withstand applied loads and environmental conditions. Structural engineers utilize principles of physics and mathematics to develop safe and cost-effective solutions for buildings, bridges, and other infrastructure projects. Their expertise ensures structural integrity, durability, and safety throughout the lifespan of a structure.
